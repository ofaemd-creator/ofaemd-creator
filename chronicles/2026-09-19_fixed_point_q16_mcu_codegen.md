# Q16.16 Fixed-Point Microcontroller Synthesis

**Author:** Dr. Ömer Faruk Aydın  
**Date:** September 19, 2026  
**Domain:** Embedded Systems & Edge Computing  

## Fixed-Point Representation
- 32-bit signed integer with 16 fractional bits: S15.16
- Overflow detection via saturation arithmetic.
- Eliminates hardware FPU dependency on Cortex-M0/M3 microcontrollers.
