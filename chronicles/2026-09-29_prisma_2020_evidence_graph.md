# PRISMA 2020 Evidence Architecture & Integrity Enforcement

**Author:** Dr. Ömer Faruk Aydın  
**Date:** September 29, 2026  
**Domain:** Medical Evidence Synthesis  

## Zero-Fabrication Directive
Automated systematic reviews must track every candidate record:
1. Identification (PubMed / CrossRef API with verifiable query and date).
2. Screening (Abstract & title relevance screening with explicit inclusion criteria).
3. Eligibility (Full-text analysis with documented exclusion reasons).
4. Included (Verified studies included in quantitative synthesis).

No synthetic data, fabricated effect sizes, or artificial DOI references are ever permitted.
